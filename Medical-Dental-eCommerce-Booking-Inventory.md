## Project Overview

The **Medical & Dental Ecommerce Website with Booking, Inventory & Stripe Payments** is a healthcare-focused ecommerce application designed for medical suppliers, dental businesses, pharmacies, clinics and professional healthcare service providers.

The system combines product sales, inventory administration, service booking requests, customer accounts, online checkout and Stripe payment integration.

It also includes a centralized administration dashboard, product categories, brands, customer orders, product reviews, blogs, buying guides, service pages and website settings.

The project is designed as a responsive platform suitable for businesses planning an online medical or dental product store, particularly those targeting the United Kingdom.
